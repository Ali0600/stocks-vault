# ServiceNow Q2 2026 — AI crosses $1B in annual contract value, RPO $29B

## Summary
[[NOW]]'s Q2 2026 beat the high end of its own guidance on every topline and
profitability metric: subscription revenues of $3,877M (+24.5%) and total revenues
of $3,987M (+24%), with remaining performance obligations of $29.0B (+21%) and
current RPO of $13.20B (+21%).

The routable disclosure is that **ServiceNow AI crossed $1 billion in annual
contract value** in the quarter — a concrete revenue figure attached to AI
products rather than a claim about adoption — and management said agentic
deployments rose ninefold in nine months. The company also disclosed 123
transactions over $1M in net-new ACV (+~40% YoY) and 658 customers above $5M in
ACV (+~23%).

Notably, the gross-margin guidance is explained by "more customers utilizing our
hyperscaler partnerships and an acceleration of customer AI adoption" — AI
delivery is running on third-party cloud capacity, so ServiceNow's AI growth
converts into hyperscaler consumption rather than into its own infrastructure.

## Claims & Verification
- Total revenues $3,987M (+24%, +22.5% constant currency) — **verified** (yfinance
  quarterly income statement, period 2026-06-30: $3.987B).
- Subscription revenues $3,877M (+24.5%, +23% cc) — **company-reported** (yfinance
  does not split subscription from total).
- GAAP diluted EPS $0.29 and GAAP net income $298M — **verified** (yfinance:
  $0.29 and $298M). The release leads on subscription revenue and RPO rather than
  GAAP earnings; the GAAP figures are the smaller number here.
- Remaining performance obligations $29.0B (+21%, +22% cc); current RPO $13.20B
  (+21%, +21.5% cc) — **company-reported**.
- ServiceNow AI crossed $1B in annual contract value in Q2 2026 —
  **company-reported**.
- Agentic deployments of ServiceNow AI increased ninefold in nine months —
  **company-reported**.
- 123 transactions over $1M in net-new ACV (+~40% YoY); 658 customers with more
  than $5M in ACV (+~23% YoY) — **company-reported**.
- Q2 subscription revenues exceeded the high end of guidance by 150bps, partly
  because strong U.S. Federal demand pulled some on-premise subscription revenue
  forward from Q3 into Q2 — **company-reported** (a pull-forward, so it flatters
  Q2 at Q3's expense).
- Q3 2026 guidance: subscription revenues $3,975–3,980M (+20.5%, +20% cc), cRPO
  growth 19.5% (+20% cc), non-GAAP operating margin 31% — **company guidance**.
- Full-year 2026 guidance raised: subscription revenues $15,760–15,780M (+22.5%,
  +21% cc), non-GAAP subscription gross margin 81%, operating margin 31.5%, free
  cash flow margin 35% — **company guidance**.
- An estimated ~$35M year-over-year FX headwind to Q3 2026 cRPO —
  **company guidance**.
