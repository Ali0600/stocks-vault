# Micron FQ1 2026 — record quarter as AI memory demand accelerates

## Summary
[[MU]]'s fiscal Q1 2026 (quarter ended 2025-11-27) was a record: revenue $13.64B,
up from $11.32B the prior quarter and $8.71B a year earlier, with GAAP diluted EPS
of $4.60. Every business unit expanded margin — the Cloud Memory unit alone did
$5.28B at a 66% gross margin, roughly double its year-ago revenue.

The quarter matters to the vault because it is the first hard, company-sourced
confirmation of the [[HBM]] thesis that MU's note had only carried as third-party
reporting: the AI-driven segments (Cloud Memory, Core Data Center) are where the
margin expansion is concentrated, and management guided FQ2 revenue to $18.70B —
a further ~37% sequential step. Micron also declared a $0.115 quarterly dividend
and posted its highest-ever free cash flow ($3.91B adjusted).

## Claims & Verification
- Revenue $13.64B ($13,643M), vs $11,315M prior quarter and $8,709M a year ago —
  **verified** (yfinance quarterly income statement, period 2025-11-30: $13.643B).
- GAAP gross margin $7,646M / 56.0% of revenue — **verified** (yfinance gross
  profit $7.646B).
- GAAP operating income $6,136M / 45.0% — **verified** (yfinance: $6.136B).
- GAAP net income $5,240M, diluted EPS $4.60 — **verified** (yfinance net income
  $5.240B, diluted EPS $4.60).
- Non-GAAP net income $5,482M, EPS $4.78 — **company-reported** (an issuer-defined
  adjusted measure; yfinance carries only the GAAP figure).
- Business-unit revenue: Cloud Memory $5,284M (66% GM), Core Data Center $2,379M
  (51%), Mobile and Client $4,255M (54%), Automotive and Embedded $1,720M (45%) —
  **company-reported** (segment splits are not in yfinance).
- Operating cash flow $8.41B; capital expenditures net $4,505M; adjusted free cash
  flow $3,906M — **company-reported**.
- Quarterly dividend of $0.115/share, payable 2026-01-14 to holders of record
  2025-12-29 — **company-reported**.
- FQ2-26 guidance: revenue $18.70B ± $400M, GAAP gross margin 67.0% ± 1.0%, GAAP
  diluted EPS $8.19 ± $0.20 — **company guidance**.
