# Robinhood Q2 2026 — record $1.31B revenue, event contracts up 10x

## Summary
[[HOOD]]'s Q2 2026 set records: total net revenues of $1.31B (+32%), diluted EPS of
$0.62 (+48%), net deposits of $21.7B and 4.8 million Gold subscribers. Total
platform assets reached $369B (+32%) and funded customers 28.4 million (+7%).

The composition is the interesting part for the diversification thesis the vault
already tracks on this name. Transaction revenue rose 44% to $776M, but the growth
is concentrated in newer lines: **event contracts revenue of $156M, up more than
10x**, options $342M (+29%) and equities $129M (+95%), while **cryptocurrency
revenue fell 38% to $100M**. Robinhood now says thirteen separate business lines
have passed $100M in annualized revenue. Meanwhile "other revenues" grew 54% to
$143M on Trump Account service revenue and Gold subscriptions — recurring revenue
rather than trading-volume-dependent revenue.

Two adjustments are worth carrying: net income of $573M included **$129M of gains
from the deconsolidation of Robinhood Ventures Fund I** ($0.14 of the $0.62 EPS),
and operating expenses rose 33% partly on one-time restructuring charges from a
reduction in force announced in June 2026.

## Claims & Verification
- Total net revenues $1.31B, +32% YoY — **verified** (yfinance quarterly income
  statement, period 2026-06-30: $1.308B).
- Diluted EPS $0.62, +48% — **verified** (yfinance: $0.62).
- Net income $573M, +48% — **company-reported**; yfinance reports $561M net income
  for the period, a difference consistent with the release's separate reporting of
  amounts attributable to non-controlling interests following the RVI
  deconsolidation.
- Revenue mix: transaction-based $776M (+44%), of which event contracts $156M
  (up over 10x), options $342M (+29%), equities $129M (+95%), cryptocurrencies
  $100M (−38%); net interest $389M (+9%); other $143M (+54%) —
  **company-reported**.
- Net income included $129M of gains primarily from the deconsolidation of
  Robinhood Ventures Fund I, worth $0.14 of diluted EPS — **company-reported**.
- Total operating expenses $734M (+33%), driven by marketing and growth spend,
  one-time restructuring charges from the June 2026 reduction in force, and Trump
  Accounts / Rothera expenses; adjusted opex + SBC (non-GAAP) $641M (+23%) —
  **company-reported**.
- Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) $741M, +35% — **company-reported**.
- Funded customers 28.4M (+7%); investment accounts 29.9M (+9%); total platform
  assets $369B (+32%); net deposits $21.7B (28% annualized); Gold subscribers 4.8M
  — **company-reported**.
- Thirteen business lines have reached $100M or more in annualized revenues —
  **company-reported**.
