# Intel Q2 2026 — fastest revenue growth in 15 years, and an $11B loss that isn't operational

## Summary
[[INTC]]'s Q2 2026 pairs the company's strongest revenue growth in more than
fifteen years with a large GAAP loss that has nothing to do with operations.
Revenue was $16.1B, up 25% year-over-year, with GAAP gross margin of 40.4% (up
12.9 points) and GAAP operating margin of 11.1% — up 35.8 points from a Q2 2025
operating *loss*. Non-GAAP net income was $2.2B ($0.42/share).

The headline GAAP loss of $11.0B (−$2.16/share) is driven almost entirely by a
**$12.5B non-cash mark-to-market charge on "Escrowed Shares"** — a derivative
liability recognized under the Warrant and Common Stock Agreement with the U.S.
Government, covering Intel shares held in escrow for the Department of Commerce
under the CHIPS Act Secure Enclave agreement. Because the liability is marked to
Intel's own share price, a rising stock mechanically produces a larger loss. This
is a structural quirk of Intel's government arrangement that will keep recurring,
and it is worth understanding before reading any Intel GAAP bottom line.

Underneath it, the segment mix is the part that speaks to the AI thesis: **Data
Center and AI revenue grew 59%** to $6.3B and **Intel Foundry grew 31%** to $5.8B,
while Intel launched Xeon 6+ — its first server-class product built on Intel 18A.

## Claims & Verification
- Revenue $16.1B, up 25% YoY — **verified** (yfinance quarterly income statement,
  period 2026-06-30: $16.128B).
- GAAP diluted EPS attributable to Intel −$2.16 — **verified** (yfinance: −$2.16).
- GAAP net loss attributable to Intel $11.0B — **verified** (yfinance net income
  −$11.033B).
- GAAP gross margin 40.4%, up 12.9 points from 27.5% — **verified** (yfinance gross
  profit $6.509B on $16.128B revenue = 40.4%).
- GAAP operating income $1,796M / 11.1% margin — **company-reported**; yfinance
  reports $1.966B for the same period, a $170M gap that equals the release's
  "restructuring and other charges" line, i.e. a definitional difference in what
  each source puts inside operating income, not a discrepancy.
- Non-GAAP net income $2.2B, EPS $0.42 — **company-reported**.
- The GAAP-to-non-GAAP bridge is dominated by a $12,529M "(Gains) losses on
  mark-to-market of Escrowed Shares" charge; the release defines Escrowed Shares as
  Intel common stock held in escrow for release to the U.S. Department of Commerce
  under the CHIPS Act Secure Enclave agreement, recognized as a derivative
  liability under the Warrant and Common Stock Agreement with the U.S. Government —
  **company-reported**.
- Segment revenue: Client Computing and Physical AI Group $8.9B (+13%), Data Center
  and AI $6.3B (+59%), total Intel Products $15.1B (+28%), Intel Foundry $5.8B
  (+31%) — **company-reported**.
- Year-over-year comparability is affected by the deconsolidation of Altera
  (51% sold, effective 2025-09-12) — **company-reported**.
- Launched Xeon 6+, Intel's first server-class product on Intel 18A —
  **company-reported**.
- Demonstrated rack-scale inference infrastructure with SambaNova and Foxconn;
  a separate disaggregated agentic cloud combines Xeon, SambaNova RDUs and NVIDIA
  Blackwell GPUs — **company-reported**.
- Q3 2026 guidance: revenue $15.8–16.8B, GAAP gross margin 41.0% (non-GAAP 42.0%),
  GAAP diluted EPS $0.31 (non-GAAP $0.38) — **company guidance**.
