# Micron FQ2 2026 — revenue nearly doubles sequentially, dividend raised 30%

## Summary
[[MU]]'s fiscal Q2 2026 (quarter ended 2026-02-26) beat its own guidance by a wide
margin: revenue $23.86B against a $18.70B ± $400M guide, with GAAP diluted EPS of
$12.07 versus a guided $8.19 ± $0.20. Revenue nearly doubled sequentially and
tripled year-over-year ($8.05B), and gross margin reached 74.4% of revenue.

The composition is the part that matters for the [[HBM]] thesis: Core Data Center
revenue more than doubled sequentially ($2,379M → $5,687M) and Mobile and Client
jumped from 54% to 79% gross margin — margin expansion has spread beyond the
AI-specific segments to the whole memory portfolio, which is the signature of an
industry-wide supply shortage rather than a product-mix effect. Management cited
"tight industry supply" directly, raised the dividend 30%, and guided FQ3 revenue
to $33.5B.

## Claims & Verification
- Revenue $23.86B ($23,860M), vs $13,643M prior quarter and $8,053M a year ago —
  **verified** (yfinance quarterly income statement, period 2026-02-28: $23.860B).
- GAAP gross margin $17,755M / 74.4% of revenue — **verified** (yfinance gross
  profit $17.755B).
- GAAP operating income $16,135M / 67.6% — **verified** (yfinance: $16.135B).
- GAAP net income $13,785M, diluted EPS $12.07 — **verified** (yfinance net income
  $13.785B, diluted EPS $12.07).
- Non-GAAP net income $14,021M, EPS $12.20 — **company-reported**.
- Beat the FQ2 guidance issued 2025-12-17 (revenue $18.70B ± $400M, GAAP EPS
  $8.19 ± $0.20) — **verified** against [[2025-12-17 mu-fq1-26-earnings]].
- Business-unit revenue: Cloud Memory $7,749M (74% GM), Core Data Center $5,687M
  (74%), Mobile and Client $7,711M (79%), Automotive and Embedded $2,708M (68%) —
  **company-reported**.
- Operating cash flow $11.90B; capex net $5,004M; adjusted free cash flow $6,899M;
  cash and investments $16.7B at quarter end — **company-reported**.
- Board approved a 30% increase in the quarterly dividend, to $0.15/share, payable
  2026-04-15 to holders of record 2026-03-30 — **company-reported**.
- FQ3-26 guidance: revenue $33.5B ± $750M, gross margin ~81%, GAAP diluted EPS
  $18.90 ± $0.40 — **company guidance**.
