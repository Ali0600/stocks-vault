# Micron FQ3 2026 — HBM4 in volume, 84.6% gross margin, multi-year customer agreements

## Summary
[[MU]]'s fiscal Q3 2026 (quarter ended 2026-05-28) delivered revenue of $41.46B —
against a $33.5B ± $750M guide — at an 84.6% GAAP gross margin, with diluted EPS
of $24.67 versus a guided $18.90 ± $0.40. Revenue is now roughly 4.5x the
year-ago quarter ($9.30B).

Two things in this release change what the vault knows about Micron beyond the
numbers. First, **[[HBM]]4 is in high-volume shipment** for a lead customer's
platform, with qualification samples out to multiple end-customers and HBM4E
(1-gamma) targeted for calendar 2027 volume — the FQ3-26 release is the point
where MU's HBM4 status moves from "validating" to shipping. Second, Micron says it
has executed **multi-year Strategic Customer Agreements** that management expects
to "enhance the durability and predictability" of results; the release names no
counterparties and discloses no terms, but a commodity memory maker converting to
multi-year committed volume is a structural change to how cyclical the business is.

The company guided FQ4-26 revenue to $50.0B ± $1.0B at ~86% gross margin.

## Claims & Verification
- Revenue $41.46B ($41,456M), vs $23,860M prior quarter and $9,301M a year ago —
  **verified** (yfinance quarterly income statement, period 2026-05-31: $41.456B).
- GAAP gross margin $35,056M / 84.6% of revenue — **verified** (yfinance gross
  profit $35.056B).
- GAAP operating income $33,318M / 80.4% — **verified** (yfinance: $33.318B).
- GAAP net income $28,243M, diluted EPS $24.67 — **verified** (yfinance net income
  $28.243B, diluted EPS $24.67).
- Non-GAAP net income $28,857M, EPS $25.11 — **company-reported** (the issuer's
  adjusted measure; the GAAP figure is the one yfinance carries).
- Beat the FQ3 guidance issued 2026-03-18 (revenue $33.5B ± $750M, GAAP EPS
  $18.90 ± $0.40) — **verified** against [[2026-03-18 mu-fq2-26-earnings]].
- Business-unit revenue: Cloud Memory $13,769M (83% GM), Core Data Center $11,524M
  (87%), Mobile and Client $11,521M (87%), Automotive and Embedded $4,634M (79%) —
  **company-reported**.
- Operating cash flow $25.39B; capex net $7,084M; adjusted free cash flow $18,304M;
  cash, marketable investments and restricted cash $30.2B at quarter end —
  **company-reported**.
- "Micron executes transformational Strategic Customer Agreements" (headline); CEO
  cites "multi-year Strategic Customer Agreements" — **company-reported**;
  counterparties, volumes, and duration are **not disclosed** in the release.
- HBM4 on 1-beta DRAM in high-volume shipments for the lead customer's platform;
  qualification samples shipped to multiple end-customers; HBM4E on 1-gamma in
  development for calendar-2027 volume — **company-reported**.
- Other product milestones: 256GB DDR5 RDIMM qualification samples (1-gamma, 3D
  die stacking); LP5X SOCAMM2 in high-volume production; G9 PCIe Gen6 SSD in
  high-volume production; 245TB QLC SSD shipping; 1-gamma 16Gb LPDDR5X ramping at
  a leading smartphone OEM; first 1-gamma DDR5 samples to a robotaxi customer —
  **company-reported**.
- Quarterly dividend $0.15/share, payable 2026-07-21 to holders of record
  2026-07-06 — **company-reported**.
- FQ4-26 guidance: revenue $50.0B ± $1.0B, gross margin ~86%, GAAP diluted EPS
  $30.73 ± $1.00 — **company guidance**.
