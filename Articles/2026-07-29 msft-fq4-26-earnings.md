# Microsoft FQ4 2026 — Azure +43%, commercial backlog $678B, first $100B Azure year

## Summary
[[MSFT]]'s fiscal Q4 2026 (quarter ended 2026-06-30) delivered revenue of $90.0B
(+18%), operating income of $40.6B (+18%) and GAAP diluted EPS of $4.81 (+32%).
Microsoft Cloud revenue was $59.3B (+27%), and Azure and other cloud services grew
43%.

Two disclosures matter for the vault's AI-infrastructure picture. First,
**commercial remaining performance obligation rose 84% to $678B** — contracted
future revenue nearly doubling is the demand signal that sits underneath the
capex the whole [[AI Supply Chain]] depends on. Second, Azure passed **$100B in
annual revenue** for the first time and Microsoft 365 Copilot passed **30 million
paid seats**, which is the clearest public evidence so far that AI capacity is
being paid for by end customers rather than only being built.

The quarter also carries a distinctive accounting feature: Microsoft now reports a
non-GAAP measure that strips out the impact of its OpenAI investment, which swung
GAAP net income by $2.06B year-over-year. GAAP net income rose 31%; adjusted for
OpenAI it rose 22%.

## Claims & Verification
- Revenue $90.0B (+18%, +17% constant currency) — **verified** (yfinance quarterly
  income statement, period 2026-06-30: $90.007B).
- Operating income $40.6B (+18%) — **verified** (yfinance: $40.603B).
- Net income $35.8B (+31% GAAP) — **verified** (yfinance: $35.766B).
- Diluted EPS $4.81 (+32% GAAP) — **verified** (yfinance: $4.81).
- Non-GAAP net income $35.3B / EPS $4.74 (+22% / +23%), excluding the OpenAI
  investment impact — **company-reported** (an issuer-defined adjustment).
- Microsoft Cloud revenue $59.3B, +27% — **company-reported** (not a yfinance line).
- Commercial remaining performance obligation +84% to $678B — **company-reported**.
- Azure and other cloud services revenue +43%; Azure surpassed $100B in annual
  revenue for the first time — **company-reported**.
- Microsoft 365 Copilot surpassed 30 million paid seats — **company-reported**.
- Segment revenue: Productivity and Business Processes $37.8B (+14%), Intelligent
  Cloud $39.3B (+32%), More Personal Computing $12.9B (−4%) — **company-reported**.
- Discrete items benefited diluted EPS by $0.27 versus the April guidance,
  including a $3.2B gain on the investment in Anthropic, partly offset by XBOX
  severance and impairment charges — **company-reported**.
- Returned $10.2B to shareholders in dividends and share repurchases —
  **company-reported**.
