# IBM Q2 2026 — software and distributed infrastructure carry a shrinking mainframe

## Summary
[[IBM]]'s Q2 2026 revenue was $17.16B with GAAP diluted EPS of $2.27. The segment
detail is where the story is: Software grew 5% to $7.8B (Red Hat +11%, Data +19%),
Consulting was flat at $5.3B, and Infrastructure fell 7% to $3.8B — but that
decline hides a split, with **IBM Z down 42%** while **Distributed Infrastructure
rose 37%**, the latter carrying an order backlog of nearly $500M in Power and
Storage.

That divergence is the routable insight: IBM's mainframe cycle is rolling over
while the AI-adjacent parts of infrastructure (Power, Storage) are growing at a
record pace. Management updated full-year expectations to constant-currency
revenue growth of 4–5% and reiterated free cash flow up about $1B year-over-year.

## Claims & Verification
- Revenue $17.16B — **verified** (yfinance quarterly income statement, period
  2026-06-30: $17.162B).
- GAAP diluted EPS $2.27 — **verified** (yfinance: $2.27).
- GAAP net income $2.165B and operating income $2.845B — **verified** (yfinance:
  $2.165B and $2.845B).
- Segment revenue: Software $7.8B (+5%) with Hybrid Cloud/Red Hat +11%, Automation
  +4%, Data +19%, Transaction Processing −8%; Consulting $5.3B (flat, +1% cc);
  Infrastructure $3.8B (−7%) with IBM Z −42% and Distributed Infrastructure +37%;
  Financing $0.2B (+12%) — **company-reported**.
- Power and Storage grew at a record pace, building an order backlog of nearly
  $500M — **company-reported**.
- Cash: net cash from operating activities $2.6B (up $0.9B YoY); free cash flow
  $2.5B (down $0.3B YoY); $1.6B returned in dividends — **company-reported**.
- Full-year 2026 expectations: constant-currency revenue growth of 4–5% (currency
  expected neutral at current rates); free cash flow up about $1B year-over-year —
  **company guidance**.
