# Micron Technology, Inc. (MU)

## Overview
Micron is the only US-based maker of memory and storage (DRAM and NAND) —
historically a brutally cyclical commodity business, now repriced as an AI story
because it is one of just three makers of high-bandwidth memory ([[HBM]]). Fiscal
2026 has been a step-change: revenue went $13.6B → $23.9B → $41.5B across FQ1–FQ3
as gross margin climbed from 56% to 85%, and management has begun converting the
business to multi-year customer agreements. Watchlist note: entered the vault as
AI-supply-chain research, not a portfolio position.

## What's Unique
Memory is normally a commodity, but HBM — the stacked DRAM that sits on the GPU
package — has turned a slice of it into a supply-constrained oligopoly. Micron is
one of only three companies on Earth that can make HBM, and the only one
headquartered in the US.

### Only They Do
- One of only three HBM manufacturers worldwide (with SK Hynix and Samsung), and the only US-based memory maker — a near-impossible business to enter, given 18–36-month, multi-billion-dollar capacity cycles
- HBM3E qualified into [[NVDA]]'s accelerators; HBM4 in high-volume shipment as of FQ3-26 with HBM4E targeted for calendar 2027; HBM capacity reported sold out through calendar 2026
- **Chokepoint:** High — AI accelerators can't run at full speed without HBM adjacent to the compute die, only three suppliers make it, and there is no spot market; Micron is #3 by share (~20%, behind SK Hynix's ~55–60%), so it is both part of a genuine bottleneck and the strategic US-based second source

## Competitors
